# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Technical founders, software engineers, and product managers who speak faster than they type. They need to turn unstructured, spontaneous voice thoughts (including Indian English and mixed vernacular terms) into concrete, engineering-ready PRDs, implementation tasks, and architecture blueprints.

## Product Purpose
Sutra captures unscripted voice rambles and turns them into production-ready specifications, actionable task sequences, and interactive architecture diagrams. It eliminates the blank-page paralysis of PRD authoring and aligns teams on technical scope before code is written.

## Positioning
Unlike generic LLM wrappers that output superficial bullet points, Sutra pairs real-time Indic voice recognition with an interactive clarifying question loop and high-speed Groq inference. It produces structured specifications with explicit edge cases, data structures, and rendered architectural diagrams.

## Core Capabilities
- **Voice Rambling:** One-click audio recording powered by Gnani.ai and browser speech engines.
- **Clarification Loop:** Smart detection of ambiguous requirements with interactive chip responses.
- **Specification Compilation:** Complete technical PRD generation with user stories, constraints, and prompt-ready outputs.
- **Architecture Blueprints:** Automatic Mermaid diagram synthesis (flowcharts, sequence, C4 components) with instant SVG rendering.
- **Tactile Multi-Theme Experience:** Handcrafted chunky editorial design with live theme switching across Carnival, Brutal, Sticker, Werkstatt, and Dark modes.

## Brand Principles
- **No AI Slop:** Authentic editorial typography, deliberate layouts, and physical button physics instead of generic purple SaaS gradients.
- **Clarity over Cleverness:** Direct verbs, crisp labels, zero corporate fluff.
- **Instant Usability:** Everything is inspectable, copyable in one click, and exportable as Markdown.
