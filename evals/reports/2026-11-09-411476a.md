## 6. Honest Limitations (Per docs/EVAL.md Section 8)

In accordance with project principles, the following limitations must be stated:
1. **Dataset Scope:** The dataset consists of 40 author-curated transcripts. While covering 10 distinct linguistic phenomena across 4 language varieties, real-world conversational rambling spans broader vocabulary and dialect variation.
2. **Single Annotator:** Initial gold labels were created by an expert developer author and verified. Inter-annotator agreement (Cohen's kappa) should be measured in future iterations with a second annotator.
3. **Topic Alignment:** Topic keys are system-chosen strings. Evaluation uses semantic token-overlap matching to align predicted and gold decisions; highly novel phrasings may affect exact match precision.
4. **Domain Focus:** All transcripts represent software feature dictations and product build briefs; results should not be generalized to legal or medical dictation without targeted domain adaptation.
